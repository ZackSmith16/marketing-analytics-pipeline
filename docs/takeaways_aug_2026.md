# Key Takeaways: August 2026

All figures come from the pipeline (BigQuery marts) for Aug 1–31, 2026 versus Aug 2025 (YoY) and July 2026 (PoP).

---

## Summit Outdoor Supply

**Check values (Overview):** Spend $19,986 · Revenue $61,541 · ROAS 308% · Conversions 470 · Cost per Conversion $42.53

### Overview
Key Takeaways: August 2026
- Revenue grew 21.1% YoY to $61,541 on 16.0% more spend, so ROAS improved to 308%.
- Google Ads drove 68% of revenue at 365% ROAS; Meta returned 230%.
- Cost per conversion fell to $42.53 (from $43.80 in July).
- Next: scale Google Shopping into fall camping season and test new Meta prospecting audiences.

### Meta Ads
Key Takeaways: Meta Ads, August 2026
- Meta drove $19,429 in revenue at 230% ROAS on $8,437 spend.
- Retargeting (site visitors, 30 days) returned 544% ROAS at $24.85 per conversion.
- Lookalike prospecting returned 134% at $94.84 per conversion, and takes 76% of Meta spend.
- Next: refresh lookalike creative and shift budget toward retargeting while its ROAS holds.

### Google Ads
Key Takeaways: Google Ads, August 2026
- Google drove $42,112 in revenue at 365% ROAS; cost per conversion improved 4.1% YoY to $35.88.
- Brand Search returned 737% ROAS at $16.42 per conversion.
- Shopping (Camping Gear) is the volume driver: $26,558 revenue at 281% ROAS, 82% of Google spend.
- Next: launch Performance Max | Winter in October ahead of cold-weather demand.

### Shopify
Key Takeaways: Shopify, August 2026
- Net sales grew 6.4% YoY to $69,367 on 570 orders, with a $121.70 AOV.
- Trekking Poles ($24,653), Camp Stove Kits ($17,920) and Down Sleeping Bags ($14,454) led sales.
- 63% of orders came from new customers. Returns rose to 4.7% of gross sales, from 1.8% last August.
- Next: bundle stoves with fuel and accessories to lift AOV, and email past buyers before winter.

### Google Analytics
Key Takeaways: Google Analytics, August 2026
- Sessions grew 11.4% YoY to 29,536, with 59% engaged and a 1.79% conversion rate.
- Email converts best (2.52%) and has the highest engagement (70%).
- Google Ads claims $42,112 in revenue, but GA4 credits it with $23,464. Meta claims $19,429 and GA4 credits $9,942.
- Next: use GA4 and Shopify revenue for budget decisions, not platform-reported ROAS.

## Harbor Dental Group (lead gen)

**Check values (Overview):** Spend $7,937 · Leads 304 · Cost per Lead $26.11 · Revenue/ROAS $0 (no online sales)

### Overview
Key Takeaways: August 2026
- Leads grew 6.5% YoY to 304 while spend fell 20.2% to $7,937, after the Invisalign campaign ended last year.
- Cost per lead is $26.11, down from $26.25 in July.
- Meta drove 72% of leads at $11.42 each; Google leads cost $64.02.
- Next: test reallocating some Google budget to the Meta New Patient Offer while its cost per lead holds.

### Meta Ads
Key Takeaways: Meta Ads, August 2026
- Meta drove 219 leads at $11.42 each on $2,501 spend.
- All leads came from the New Patient Offer instant form, the cheapest lead source this month.
- Instant-form leads are easy to submit, so lead quality should be checked against booked appointments.
- Next: add a qualifying question to the form and track appointment rate by source.

### Google Ads
Key Takeaways: Google Ads, August 2026
- Google drove 85 leads at $64.02 each on $5,436 spend.
- Brand Search: 47 leads at $22.18, the most efficient Google campaign.
- Emergency Dentist: 38 leads at $116.21. These are high-intent patients, but expensive.
- Next: tighten Emergency Dentist keywords and ad schedule to bring cost per lead below $100.

### Google Analytics
Key Takeaways: Google Analytics, August 2026
- 7,754 sessions (flat YoY) produced 383 conversions, a 4.94% conversion rate.
- Google Ads traffic converts best (7.79%), followed by Meta (6.67%).
- Organic Search is the largest traffic source (2,588 sessions) and converts at 3.55%.
- Next: add call tracking so phone bookings show up alongside form leads.
